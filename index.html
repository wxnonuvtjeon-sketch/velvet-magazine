01 / OUR STORY

A publication
with a point of view.

VELVET is an independent fashion and lifestyle media publication based in Jakarta, Indonesia. Established in 2017, we bring together creative minds to explore the ever-evolving worlds of fashion, beauty, lifestyle, and entertainment.

Through digital storytelling and monthly print editions, we spotlight emerging talents, create meaningful conversations, and capture the people shaping contemporary culture.
